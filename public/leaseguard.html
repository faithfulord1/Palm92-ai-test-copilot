Professional control principle

Decision architecture

AI understands. Extracts and interprets unstructured documents.
Deterministic controls verify. Reconciles numbers, dates, duplicates, currency and evidence.
Humans authorise. Consequential ERP handoff remains approval-controlled and auditable.

This separation prevents an LLM from becoming the final authority on financial correctness.
