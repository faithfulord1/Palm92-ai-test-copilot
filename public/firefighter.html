Portfolio / Interview

20-second explanation

Firefighter is controlled emergency privileged access. I would test the full lifecycle: reason and approval, time-bound access, activity logging, additional-activity justification, independent controller review, evidence and final audit trail.

45-second project pitch

I built a governed Firefighter Review Copilot that analyses synthetic emergency-access sessions with transparent rules, flags scope, timing, evidence and segregation-of-duties concerns, drafts controller questions, and then stops at a human approval gate. The same project includes 18 software tests and traceability from control objective to evidence, so it demonstrates SAP GRC understanding, testing discipline and AI governance together.
