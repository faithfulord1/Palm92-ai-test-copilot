# Responsible AI

Palm92 AI Test Copilot uses AI as an accelerator, not as the final quality authority.

## Principles

- AI-generated analysis and test cases are drafts.
- Human edits reset approval to Pending.
- Sensitive actions require explicit human approval.
- Missing facts are treated as ambiguities rather than invented assumptions.
- Evidence should make outcomes independently reviewable.
- Semantic equivalence and exact-format compliance are tested separately where appropriate.
- Release and quality decisions remain human decisions.
